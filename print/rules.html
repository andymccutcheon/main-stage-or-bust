Main Stage or Bust — Rules

A 1–2 player roll-and-write. 12 weeks. Take your DIY band from VFW halls to the main stage in twelve weeks.

Setup

  • Each band takes a band sheet + pencil. Shared dice pool: 5 white d6 + 1 gold d6.
  • Mark starting resources: Cash $20 (Basement $25, Grind $15) · Fans 0 · Fame 0 · Hype 0 · Morale 5 · Van 6 · Merch 6 · Caffeine 2.
  • Pick a band name. Pick a mode (below) and difficulty: Club (standard), Basement (easy), Grind (road dice from week 2, no mercy caffeine).

Modes

  • Solo: beat the rank table. Optional Rival AI: after your week, rival takes the higher-tier offer of its level and wins on 85/65/45/30% (T1–Main); win = full venue averages, fail = half cash +3 fans.
  • Coop (2p): one shared sheet. Roll 6 dice (3 each), decide together. All difficulties +1 (the van is crowded).
  • Versus (2p): separate sheets, same 2 offers each week (both may take the same). Higher show headlines (+3 fans +$2); opener +1 fame. Tie: both +1 fan.

The week (repeat 12×)

  1. Upkeep: Manager costs $3. Roadie repairs +1 van. New week? Weeks 4, 7, 10 (Grind: also 2): roll 1d6 on the Road Table first.
  2. Offers: roll/draw 2 venues from your highest unlocked tier + the tier below (50/50 each). Main Stage can appear from week 10+ if fame ≥45 (20% chance per offer slot). Pick 1, pay entry. Discard the other.
  3. Soundcheck: roll 5 dice (+1 gold with Manager). Spend Caffeine: 1 token = reroll any dice (max 2 passes/week).
  4. Stage: place any 2 dice. Optionally spend Hype: 2 hype = +1 show (max +2/show). Show = dice + skill + albums + tech + hype-spend. Win ties.
  5. Work: assign the 3 leftover dice, one action each — Merch (sell min(die, stock) shirts × $2; Roadie +2 free), Flyer (die ≥4: +that many fans; ≥5: +1 hype), Day job (+$ = die; a 6 also +1 van).
  6. Songwriting: if leftover dice contain a pair, check 1 Song box (1/week max). 4 boxes = album: +5 fame, +10 fans, +1 permanent show hype. Four-of-a-kind anywhere in kept dice = Anthem (+5 fans, +3 fame, 1/week).
  7. Payouts: win = full cash + fame, fans = venue bonus + max(1, margin). Fail = half cash (round down), +1 fan, −1 morale. Coffee House always +1(caf). +1 skill star per win; every 4th star = +1 skill (max +3).

Fail states

  • Morale 0 → next week is forced rest: no gig; +$5, +3 morale, +1(caf).
  • Van 0 → next week is forced shop: −$4, van +3, no gig. Anytime: mechanic $4 = van +2. Restock anytime: $1 per 2 shirts.
  • Three straight fails (non-Grind): +1 mercy (caf).

Crew (buy anytime)

  • Roadie $30: +2 free merch sales/show, van +1 each upkeep. Guitar tech $35: +1 show hype. Manager $25 + $3/wk: roll the gold die.

Scoring

Final = Fans + Fame + ⌊Cash ÷ 5⌋ + Albums × 10. Versus: higher total wins.

Design notes: The Main Stage (D17) needs at least +5 engine (dice max 12) — build skills, songs, and crew first. Staging your best dice competes with keeping a pair for songs. Money keeps the van alive; fame unlocks rooms; fans win.
